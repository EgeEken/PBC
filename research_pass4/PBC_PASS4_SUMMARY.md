# PBC Pass 4 summary

The combined bit-exact runtime implementation improves full local encode/decode time. Frozen weight 0.4 survives close-rate testing as a useful content-dependent alternative, with actual main-set Q-HQ wins, but not a general high-quality frontier improvement. High-quality holdout coverage remains insufficient. The two exact allocator caches do not materially reduce full encoding cost, so broad promotion/composition was stopped. No optimized HF timing or PBC 3.1 release is established.

## What ran

- Runtime: 100 image/preset exactness pairs, 2612 complete proposal/RNG checks, 16 RGB/RGBA/mutation-path checks; six-image four-mode screen; all 25 images/four presets/four modes/three interleaved repeats (1200 primary rows).
- HF: public health/OpenAPI/idle-status GETs, reported repository/runtime metadata and nine successful immutable source/dependency text checks. Existing Space only; no remote encoding or benchmark start. Authenticated bounded existing-API integration is supplied as an unapplied two-file patch plus four private job files.
- RD: full prior bracket/dominance analysis, 106 new fresh ordinary-codec points selected by bitrate alone (at most six/image), then nearest fresh controls and the frozen weight 0.4 allocator on all 25 images/three repeats (150 rows). No prefixes, new weights, holdout tuning or model changes. Four named regressions have exact selector ledgers and existing fixed-geometry ablations.
- Cost: corrected six-image ordinary/frozen cProfile (24 rows), two exact caches and their composition; 24 initial exactness cases and 90 uninstrumented screening rows. The composite missed the 2% screen promotion gate, so no full 25-image cache timing or optional fast-runtime allocator composition was run. The negative result and predeclared gate are retained.

The immutable released source is research_pass1/release at 2e59f1b06980aa467da0cde94fa33c3f9a0d4d51. Root remains 3e9e8c94b7497ecefc5e482a66fde697dfad6e19. Current root has a different RNG; no current disabled-feature configuration is mislabeled released PBC. Existing default-output equivalence of old_pbc remains protected historical evidence, not source identity. Original Pass 1/2/3 raw baselines are reused transparently; no stale database result establishes a new speed claim.

All primary timings are local, single-core/numerical thread, warmed and repeated; encode/decode calls are timed separately from loading, original-resolution RGB scoring, parsing, hashing, disk/IPC and HTTP. Complete post-LZMA bits/source pixels determine bpp. Raw bytes, actual init/iterative counts/dimensions, expanded configs and source/model/input/environment/module identity are retained. Invalid initial allocation timing/profile rows from a discovered mode-prefix bug are archived/excluded; corrected reruns and automatic guards prevent mixing generic runtime flags into allocator-only comparisons.

## Explicit research answers

1. All three runtime mechanisms are compatible and byte/pixel/patch/RNG exact on the tested local cases, including all 100 default cases and all primary repeats. Decode remains v0 and AI-free.
2. Full local combined encode is -25.48% main/-26.59% holdout; decode -40.57%/-40.24%. Exact MSE/bpp/patches do not change. Per-preset data, extrema and repeats are retained.
3. Search reuse adds a directly measured -8.32% main/-7.86% holdout encode relative to grid+skip. Cache alone is -7.37%/-5.82% versus current. Grid+skip is -18.72%/-20.32%; no speed percentages are added. Decoder gains come from grid I/O; cache-only decode differences are noise.
4. Existing HF has a real benchmark API, but it cannot select these undeployed modes or a bounded input/preset subset. Its normal PBC minimum is 80 operations, persistent DB appends; Quick RD overwrites shared results and lacks independent decode timing/scoring.
5. Authorized current deployment checks were health/schema/status/source inspection. A full existing-codec suite is technically available but exceeds the small-smoke scope and cannot validate optimized code. Upload-based compression paths were not used.
6. No valid remote codec timing was obtained. Recorded HTTP GET durations measure requests only. No current-root remote time is relabeled optimized or compared numerically with local seconds.
7. The minimum prepared extension is proposed_hf_benchmark.patch (server.py and pbc3_benchmark.py) plus four staged job/manifest/candidate files. It reuses existing start/status/stop with a disabled-by-default secret and static bounds, executes only in a private child, appends isolated research files, and changes no production codec/UI/default. Applying/committing/pushing, secret configuration, rebuild/restart and remote job execution require explicit future authorization on the existing Space.
8. Public UI remains unchanged with internal MSE enabled; server's float(result.mse) remains valid. Private/file-only paths may skip and independently score. No metric-producing UI speedup is claimed from omitting a metric it still needs.
9. Weight0.4 survives many genuinely close actual controls as a content-dependent alternative: nearest fresh5%-matched main 13/16 MSE wins, holdout 4/4; best measured close old/new main 16/19 and holdout 3/4 wins. Signed rate gaps and strict RD wins remain separate. Being better than one fresh control is not sufficient if an older point dominates.
10. Fresh matching covers16/20 main and 4/5 holdout at 5%; 14/20 and 4/5 at 3%. Reusing existing measured close controls expands coverage to 19/20 main, 4/5 holdout. field remains unmatched; ducks is 5.0021% from its nearest fresh control, just outside tolerance. Fresh-only unmatched main images are field, flower, flowers, monkey. No additional unbounded search was performed.
11. Earlier prior interpolation is reproduced(-5.16% main/-9.26% holdout). The conservative old/densified estimate is -3.05%/-8.34%, with all 20/all 5 overlaps, but remains interpolation. Actual close-control means answer a different question and cannot be substituted for these percentages. Newly measured inefficient points can expose an old chord's optimistic shape; a weaker new chord is not automatically a stronger baseline.
12. Some main Quality-to-HQ finite operating points improve against actual close controls; broad high-quality holdout improvement is unproven. Main fresh-close Q-HQ subset has 12 cases/9 MSE wins. Only one holdout Q-HQ case has a 5% actual match, essentially neutral/slightly worse than the strongest nearby point. Candidate rates outside that interval support only an all-rate conclusion.
13. Gains are content-dependent. Two main candidates and one holdout candidate are dominated by an observed control somewhere on the measured curve. Large direct gains against low-budget500k controls do not repair mario64/pigeon losses versus an existing lower-resolution point. Five holdout images do not establish robust content generalization.
14. mario64: every one of 35 actions changes representation (30 coarser, five lower precision); no early stop. Changed actions retain88.1% of local gain at 34.1% raw bits on average, yet final RGB loses detail/efficiency versus 250k Balanced, which already has both lower rate and MSE. The fixed-geometry counterpart is worse still, so later evolving geometry partially recovers quality at additional rate; it does not erase the observed dominance. Coarsening and resolution/allocation interaction are supported mechanisms; an isolated percentage of final loss caused by high-frequency detail is not identified. Monet:18/35 representations change (eight coarser, ten lower precision); the allocator runs35 patches versus ordinary33. Changed actions retain84.0% local gain/51.1% raw bits. This is not premature stopping. Unlike the other named cases, new close ordinary controls favor the allocator(-8.64% measured MSE against the best5%-near point); the prior interpolated envelope still predicts+12.02% loss. It remains an interpolation/control-family issue rather than a newly verified direct loss, and must not be called fixed or assigned one precision-only cause. Eiffel: nine of 20 emitted actions change (seven lower precision, two coarser), retaining 93.65% local gain/70.65% raw bits. The modified trajectory stops at 20 versus ordinary23; both final MSE and bpp worsen (25.22/1.82813 to 27.44/1.88780). A fresh p25 control at 1.81396 bpp/25.04869 MSE dominates it; this is a verified representation/trajectory/stop failure, not merely a sparse interpolation artifact. Pigeon: all 35 actions change (20 coarser, 15 lower precision), without early stopping, retaining 90.95% local gain/51.76% raw bits. It beats a nearby low-budget500k control but is dominated by released Balanced at 0.009133 bpp/51.3971 MSE versus 0.012250/53.6133. Its main failure is extra500k representation cost relative to an effective low-rate250k trajectory, with coarsening/precision and subsequent geometry interacting. No unique model-capacity or entropy-statistic cause is proved for any case.
15. Another fixed-weight sweep is not justified. Raw proxy/local-SSE, later geometry/stop behavior and original RGB/final entropy interact. Content-aware allocation is a hypothesis requiring independent data; no classifier was implemented. The experiment does not isolate a fundamental representation-capacity limit.
16. The same-runtime uninstrumented six-image screen measures ordinary0.76551 s versus frozen allocator0.82826 s: incremental62.75 ms/+8.20%. Corrected cProfile shows extra representations plus more actual search/model steps; local candidate work alone cannot explain the whole difference. The full 25-image nearest-rate confirmation separately measures main 0.64256 s ordinary versus 0.93821 s allocator, and holdout 0.45380 s versus 0.70176 s; those controls often request fewer patches and therefore measure rate-matched operating-point compute, not equal35-budget allocator-only overhead.

Projection+before-SSE gives0.82083 s versus frozen0.82826 s(-0.90%) on six main images/three repeats; projection alone+1.04%, before-SSE -0.41%. 18. Exact cache variants preserve all frozen allocator decisions/files, so their RD advantages and failures remain unchanged; this is runtime attribution, not a new RD result.
19. Combined fast-runtime allocator cost was not measured: the cache composite failed its predeclared substantial-saving gate, so the optional broad composition was stopped. The independently confirmed fast runtime remains valid, but its percentages are not added to allocation timings or used to infer an unmeasured release/HF cost. A already measures the combined runtime without allocation; C's only allocator cost result is the same-underlying-runtime six-image screen.
20. Allocation remains plausible research for PBC 3.1, but is not an accepted all-metric release improvement. Content regressions, incomplete high-quality holdout coverage, cost and undeployed HF behavior remain gates.
21. Prioritize independent images with actual tightly matched ordinary controls specifically in the Quality-to-HQ region, keeping the allocator frozen and retaining the named dominance failures; this is the remaining RD validation gap. Separately, obtain explicit authorization for the minimal bounded benchmark extension on the existing HF Space and validate grid/search/metric-free runtime modes in one worker before any production change. Only pursue one targeted representation/geometry hypothesis if these direct high-quality results justify it; a classifier or more fixed weights is not yet supported. A PBC 4 rewrite or broad native decoder effort is lower priority than these measured gaps.
22. Bit-exact NumPy grid I/O and exact search reuse are ready for production review/HF consideration with explicit owned state and same-runtime validation. Existing MSE opt-out is suitable only for callers that do not require its value. No candidate has authorization for production installation here.
23. Stop broad fixed-weight/entropy/RL sweeps, shipping expensive fixed-geometry replay, and claiming whole-codec gains from local utility/microtimings. Do not introduce a classifier, broad native rewrite or PBC 4 representation until the measured frontier/capacity evidence justifies it. The two screened allocator cache mechanisms should stop at their negative substantial-saving gate; do not spend a full 25-image timing suite trying to turn a0.9% point estimate into a release claim.

## Classification and next steps

Verified local output-preserving runtime improvement: combined grid/MSE-opt-out/search reuse. RD tradeoff versus equal-budget ordinary encoder and useful content-dependent finite-frontier alternative: frozen allocator0.4. Confirmed measured dominance failures: mario64, eiffel, pigeon; Monet's direct and interpolated comparisons disagree and remain explicitly separate. High-quality holdout generalization and optimized HF CPU benefit: inconclusive/unvalidated. Allocator cache cost reduction: exact but too small for promotion, with projection's point estimate regressing. No strict all-metric PBC 3.1 improvement or production acceptance.

Prioritize independent images with actual tightly matched ordinary controls specifically in the Quality-to-HQ region, keeping the allocator frozen and retaining the named dominance failures; this is the remaining RD validation gap. Separately, obtain explicit authorization for the minimal bounded benchmark extension on the existing HF Space and validate grid/search/metric-free runtime modes in one worker before any production change. Only pursue one targeted representation/geometry hypothesis if these direct high-quality results justify it; a classifier or more fixed weights is not yet supported. A PBC 4 rewrite or broad native decoder effort is lower priority than these measured gaps.

Observations are actual decoded files/metrics, direct measured rates, proposal/RNG checks and complete call walls. Causal evidence is paired runtime flags, frozen exact cache output, and existing fixed/evolving geometry ablations. Attribution of final-RGB losses to fine detail versus precision/entropy beyond these diagnostics remains a hypothesis. No improvement is accepted merely because a proxy looks efficient.
