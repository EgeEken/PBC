# Pass 8

The actual immutable PBC 3.0 release and actual integrated development defaults were measured on the same existing HF cpu-basic worker: 20 main images × four presets × two implementations × three interleaved trials, with public internal MSE enabled. Each implementation retains its authentic RNG. Complete speed, RD, variation and 80 per-image outcomes are in [PBC30_VS_DEV31_HF_BENCHMARK.md](PBC30_VS_DEV31_HF_BENCHMARK.md).

Aggregate encode/decode time reductions by preset: Compression **21.99%/19.78%**, Balanced **18.33%/35.33%**, Quality **24.85%/46.86%**, HQ **31.87%/67.19%**. Overall **24.80%/48.39%**, with 72/80 encode and 77/80 decode wins. Eiffel Balanced encodes 38.70% slower; named regressions remain visible. These percentages are direct release-to-development measurements, not the earlier preceding-HF ratios.

Overall MSE is **0.763% higher**, bpp **2.670% higher**, and mean total patches 28.55 → 29.3875. There are nine strict RD wins and twelve strict losses. Encoding/decoding speed improve in aggregate; quality, bitrate, universal speed wins and preservation of every extreme-compression point have not been achieved. There is no four-axis dominance or PBC 3.1 release claim.

Robust palette bounds were investigated only after those mandatory outputs and UI checks were complete. Replaying 331 saved patches required no new searches. The 98th-percentile rule passed the small fixed-state gate; 95th failed. Its 72-call forward screen gave MSE −0.271%, bpp +6.983%, encode +13.496%, zero strict RD wins and seven MSE regressions. It is rejected; no full main/holdout test, retuning or RD deployment followed. See [PBC_ROBUST_PALETTE_RESULTS.md](PBC_ROBUST_PALETTE_RESULTS.md).

Recommended next work: a small release-compatible-RNG development control to isolate engineering speed from RNG-induced trajectory/RD changes; then audit the working-space-to-final-RGB distortion mismatch on saved trajectories before another local-scoring modification. Do not deepen stop fallback or sweep more palette percentiles.
